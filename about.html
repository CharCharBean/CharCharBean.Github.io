About

The narrative

I'm Charles Russell — a Managerial Economics student at UC Davis, a little obsessed with the space where people, data, and business decisions meet.

What gets me up in the morning is turning a messy, ambiguous problem into a clear recommendation someone can actually act on. As a Data & Strategy consulting intern at Highlander Consulting Group, I've dug through two years of point-of-sale data to find the exact window when a promotion would land, mapped new markets for an AI hiring startup, and built a go-to-market strategy for a biotech spinout that took 2nd place at the UCR Blackstone LaunchPad Challenge.

I started out learning to code in Computer Science at UC Riverside, and I've been chasing the "why" behind the numbers ever since — because the sharpest analysis is useless if nobody's convinced by it. I care as much about the people behind the data as the data itself, and I love the moment a spreadsheet turns into a decision.

I'm looking for roles in strategy, analytics, and consulting where I can keep learning fast and turning evidence into impact. Outside of work, I'm an avid gardener — there's no shortcut to a good garden, only small, consistent effort and patient maintenance. I've come to believe that's where success comes from too: doing the hard thing, well, for a long time.
