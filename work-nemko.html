At a Glance

Nemko is a global testing, inspection, and certification firm, and I spent the summer supporting its North American sales team. I sourced and qualified leads, got on the phone, and built an automated pipeline that scores prospects straight from the FCC's equipment authorization database. The result: 500+ new leads, 12 discovery meetings, two closed projects, and a return offer for Summer 2027.

The Context

The role

Nemko tests and certifies products before they go to market, so some of its best prospects are companies with new equipment on the way. I joined the Carlsbad office as a Sales Support Intern on the North American sales team, and my job was to keep the account executives supplied with qualified leads.

In practice that meant three things: finding the right companies, building targeted prospect lists in Salesforce and SalesIntel, and reaching out directly to get discovery meetings on the calendar. The more of that I did by hand, the clearer it got which parts could be automated.

The Work

What I worked on

01

Build the lists

Sourced and qualified outbound leads for the North American team, building targeted prospect lists in Salesforce and SalesIntel.

02

Work the phones

Ran cold-call outreach that generated 500+ new leads and booked 12 discovery meetings for account executives.

03

Automate the search

Built a lead-generation pipeline with Claude Code that scrapes, cleans, and scores prospects from the FCC equipment authorization database using pass/fail qualification criteria.
