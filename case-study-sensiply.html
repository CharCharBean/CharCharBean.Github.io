Case Study

Sensiply

Finding the verticals and the distribution channel that let a two-person team scale an AI recruiter.

Role
Consulting Intern — Data & Strategy
Timeline
Spring 2026
Client
Sensiply (via Highlander Consulting)
Focus
Market expansion
Executive Summary

Sensiply, an early-stage AI recruiter, had real product-market signal but only a two-person go-to-market team — and no clear answer on which markets and channels to concentrate on. I scored eleven verticals across five dimensions, deep-dived the top candidates, and pressure-tested the highest-leverage distribution channel against its real prerequisites. The result was a focused strategy: lead with Healthcare, unlock the staffing-agency channel through a Bullhorn Marketplace listing, and reframe positioning around the >95% text-to-call conversion rate that no competitor could claim.

The Context

The challenge

Sensiply's product was differentiated — proactive outbound voice AI, 55+ language support, autonomous 24/7 screening, and ATS-agnostic integration — and it had early traction across food service and hospitality clients. But with only two people running go-to-market, the company couldn't pursue every opportunity at once. The strategic question was focus: which verticals deserved the team's limited attention, which distribution channel could multiply a small team's reach, and how to position the product against incumbents like Paradox (acquired by Workday for roughly $1B) without competing head-on.

The stakes were sharpest in markets where hiring pain was most acute. Healthcare alone showed an average 78-day RN time-to-fill, 42% of applicants going three weeks with no recruiter contact, and roughly $5.19M in annual nursing-turnover loss per hospital. The opportunity was real, but so was the risk of spreading a two-person team too thin or entering a channel before its legal and integration prerequisites were solved.

The Framework

My approach

01

Score the verticals

Built a priority matrix scoring eleven verticals across five dimensions — hiring volume, time-to-hire pain, recruiter overload, AI adoption, and fit — to rank where Sensiply's strengths met the most urgent unmet need.

02

Deep-dive & audit

Market-sized the top verticals, mapped their tech stacks, and audited Paradox, Fountain, and HireVue to locate the positioning gap none of them owned.

03

Pressure-test the channel

Built a standalone investment thesis for the staffing-agency channel — leverage math, prerequisites, and an honest downside case — then rewrote the messaging around outcomes.

The Findings

Insights & recommendations

What the analysis showed

  • Identified three new high-turnover verticals — seasonal resort towns, healthcare, and home health — beyond the existing food-service and hospitality base.
  • The staffing-agency channel was the single highest-leverage motion for a two-person team — one agency partnership opens access to ~50 employer clients at once — but it was gated by three prerequisites: a Bullhorn Marketplace listing, a TCPA-compliant consent framework, and legacy-database consent segmentation.
  • Recommendation: Concentrate on Healthcare now while qualifying for the Bullhorn listing, and reframe the pitch around outcomes — none of the main competitors led with outbound voice or multilingual support, making the >95% text-to-call conversion rate the ownable differentiator.
The Outcome

Business impact

3

100+

13
SoCal employers served

The most valuable part of the work wasn't finding opportunities — it was deciding what to say no to. A two-person team can't win a frontal fight against a Workday-backed incumbent, so the strategy turned on focus: lead where the pain and the pipeline already aligned, and earn the distribution channel by solving its prerequisites honestly rather than overselling readiness. Translating a capable product into a sequenced, defensible go-to-market plan is where the value was created.
